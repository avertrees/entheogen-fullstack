# Impure Desire (125)

Date Created: February 8, 2023 3:37 PM

## Journaling Prompts:

**If this feeling could talk, what would it say?**

**What void are you trying to fill with your impure desire? What needs are
not being met that are causing this desire?**

## Practice:

Impure desires stem from a separation from our sense of self worth. Go sit in
front of a mirror and imagine you are looking at your child self. Tell this
younger version of you everything they need to hear to know that they are
worthy. Stay until you feel better. When you feel a shift, fill up on love,
welcoming it into your body.
