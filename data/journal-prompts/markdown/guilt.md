# Guilt (30)

Date Created: February 8, 2023 3:34 PM

## Journaling Prompts:

**What is making you feel guilty? What action can you take to practice
forgiveness and integrate the lesson, so that you can access the
wisdom in the wound causing you guilt.**

## Practice:

Movement is the number one way that we can move stagnant energy
blocks. I recommend putting on one of the playlists linked in the first
section of this resource and embodying the RELEASE of the guilt. Shake
it out. Yell it into a pillow. Sweep it with your fingertips from your body.
