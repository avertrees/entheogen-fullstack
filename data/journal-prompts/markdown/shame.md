# Shame (20)

Date Created: February 8, 2023 3:33 PM

## Journaling Prompts:

**What actions can you take to begin to connect with self-love and
confidence (the opposite of shame)?:**

## Practice:

Go sit in front of a mirror and practice seeing yourself through the eyes
of someone who fully loves and accepts you. Tell yourself whatever you
need to hear to release the shame. Move your body + dance, imagining
that you’re stepping into confidence and embracing love of self.
